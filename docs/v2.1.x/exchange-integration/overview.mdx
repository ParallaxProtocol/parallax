This section is for exchanges, custodians and payment processors adding
**LAX**, the native coin of Parallax. It covers running your own nodes,
crediting deposits, sending withdrawals, and deciding how many
confirmations a deposit needs.

Parallax is a proof-of-work chain that runs the Ethereum Virtual Machine. If
you already support Ethereum or an Ethereum Classic-style chain, most of
your tooling works unchanged: the same addresses, the same JSON-RPC, the
same transaction format and signing libraries. What changes is the part that
matters most to an exchange, **settlement**: blocks come every 10 minutes,
no block is ever final the way a proof-of-stake checkpoint is, and the safe
number of confirmations depends on how mining power is spread across the
network at the time.

## Integration checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the differences from Ethereum">
    Most integration problems come from finality, type 2 transactions and
    block headers. [Differences from Ethereum](./ethereum-differences).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run at least two nodes you control">
    One for deposits and one for withdrawals at minimum, on separate hosts,
    with RPC reachable only from your own services. [Running your nodes](./node).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test on a local chain">
    There is no public testnet. See [Testing your integration](#testing-your-integration).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Detect deposits from blocks, not from the mempool">
    Credit only transactions in blocks with a successful receipt, track them
    by block hash, and handle reorganizations. [Deposits](./deposits).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send withdrawals as signed legacy transactions">
    Manage nonces yourself, price fees with `eth_estimateSmartFee`, and
    replace stuck transactions by fee. [Withdrawals](./withdrawals).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a confirmation policy tied to live mining data">
    A fixed number is not enough on a young proof-of-work chain. Poll the
    status page and your own nodes, scale confirmations with deposit size,
    and define when deposits pause. [Confirmations](./confirmations) and
    [Network status API](./status-api).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch for releases">
    Subscribe to [GitHub releases](https://github.com/ParallaxProtocol/parallax/releases)
    and run `parallaxd version-check` from monitoring. Release notes say
    when an upgrade is mandatory. [Upgrades](./node#upgrades).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Testing your integration

There is no public testnet at the moment, so test against a chain of your
own:

- **Functional tests.** `parallaxd --dev` starts a single-node chain
  (chain ID 1337) that seals a block as soon as a transaction arrives. It
  is quick for exercising address generation, deposit detection, sweeping
  and withdrawals. See [Developer mode](/v2.1.x/parallax-client/developers/dapp/developer-mode).
- **Reorganization drill.** Run two or more nodes on a
  [private network](/v2.1.x/parallax-client/fundamentals/private-networks),
  let them mine apart, then reconnect them, and check that your scanner
  orphans and rescans the replaced blocks.
- **Mainnet.** Finish with small real deposits and withdrawals.
