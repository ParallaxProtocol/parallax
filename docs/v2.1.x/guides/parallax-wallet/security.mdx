- The wallet unlocks with your password once per session, and **locks
  itself** after 15 minutes of inactivity. Change that in **Settings →
  Security → Lock automatically**, or lock it at any time from the top
  bar. On macOS and Windows it also locks when your computer sleeps or
  its screen locks.
- Showing a recovery phrase, saving a backup, removing an account and
  changing your password always ask for your password again.
- **Settings → Security → Change password** re-encrypts every key and
  phrase. Backups saved before the change still open only with the old
  password.
- Everything the wallet keeps beside your keys, such as account names,
  contacts and the app's logs, is encrypted too.

---

## Backing up

Your wallet password protects the wallet on this computer, but it isn't a
backup. If this computer is lost or broken, what brings your accounts back
is:

- **Your recovery phrases**, written on paper.
- **Your single keys' backup files**, saved with **Save backup** in
  **Settings → Accounts**.
- **Hardware wallet accounts** are backed up by the device's own recovery
  phrase.

Read [Keeping your wallet safe](../wallets/keep-safe) for how to store
them.
