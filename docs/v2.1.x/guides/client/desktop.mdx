**Parallax Wallet** is the desktop app for holding, sending and receiving
LAX. It runs its own full Parallax node inside the app, so your balance
and your transactions are checked by your computer rather than by someone
else's server. There's no terminal and nothing else to install: download
it, open it, and it walks you through the rest.

The project lives at [github.com/ParallaxProtocol/parallax-gui](https://github.com/ParallaxProtocol/parallax-gui).

---

## Is this the right option for me?

Use **Parallax Wallet** if you want to:

- Keep your LAX in a wallet on your own computer, protected by a password only you know.
- Create or import a recovery phrase, import a private key or keystore file, or use a [Ledger or Trezor](../wallets/hardware-wallets).
- Send and receive LAX, keep an address book, and see your full history.
- Run a full node that helps the network, without touching a command line.
- Keep your keys on a computer that never goes online, with the [offline signer](#offline-signing).
- Let an [AI assistant](../ai/parallax-wallet) read the chain through your node, with every action approved by you.

Use the [**Parallax client CLI**](./setup) if you want to run a node on a
headless server, mine, or control every flag from a terminal. Parallax
Wallet doesn't mine.

## Installing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the latest release">
    Visit the [Parallax Wallet releases page](https://github.com/ParallaxProtocol/parallax-gui/releases/latest)
    and download the file for your computer:

    - **Windows (64-bit)**: `Parallax-Wallet-<version>-windows-x86_64-setup.exe`
      (installer) or `Parallax-Wallet-<version>-windows-x86_64.exe`
      (portable, runs without installing)
    - **macOS (Apple Silicon)**: `Parallax-Wallet-<version>-macos-arm64.dmg`
    - **macOS (Intel)**: `Parallax-Wallet-<version>-macos-x86_64.dmg`
    - **Linux (x86_64)**: `Parallax-Wallet-<version>-linux-x86_64.AppImage`
    - **Linux (ARM64)**: `Parallax-Wallet-<version>-linux-arm64.AppImage`

    Each release includes a `SHA256SUMS.txt` file you can use to verify
    the download.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install and open it">
    - **Windows**: run the installer, or open the portable `.exe`
      directly. The app isn't signed with a Windows certificate yet, so
      SmartScreen may say *Windows protected your PC*: choose **More
      info → Run anyway**.
    - **macOS**: open the `.dmg` and drag *Parallax Wallet* to
      *Applications*. The app isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first
      time you open it macOS may refuse. Open **System Settings → Privacy
      & Security**, scroll down, and choose **Open Anyway** next to
      Parallax Wallet.
    - **Linux**: make the AppImage executable
      (`chmod +x Parallax-Wallet-*.AppImage`) and run it. Some
      distributions need `libfuse2` installed to run AppImages.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only one copy of Parallax Wallet runs at a time: opening it again brings
the open window forward.

## First launch

The first time you open Parallax Wallet, it greets you and sets up your
node and your wallet, one question at a time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Language and how you'll use this computer">
    Pick your language (the app starts in your system's language when it
    has it), then choose **Wallet with my own node**. Choose **Offline
    signer** only for a computer that will never go online; see
    [Offline signing](#offline-signing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Where to keep your data">
    The blockchain needs room on disk. The suggested folder is fine for
    most people; see [Files on your computer](#files-on-your-computer).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Help the network">
    **Allow inbound connections** lets other nodes connect to yours,
    which strengthens the network. It's recommended, and you can turn it
    off later. Press **Start node**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it sync">
    Your node downloads the blockchain and checks it. You can wait until
    it's in sync, or press **Continue while it syncs** and carry on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your wallet password">
    At least 8 characters. It protects every key and recovery phrase in
    the wallet, and it **can't be recovered**: if you lose it, only your
    recovery phrases and backups can bring your accounts back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your recovery phrase">
    Choose one:

    - **Create recovery phrase** — the app shows you 24 words. Write them
      down on paper, then type three of them back to confirm. You can add
      an optional passphrase.
    - **Import recovery phrase** — type the 12 to 24 words of a phrase you
      already have. The app finds the accounts you've used and ticks them.
    - **Connect a hardware wallet** — use a Ledger or Trezor; see
      [Hardware wallets](../wallets/hardware-wallets).
  </Step>
</Steps>

✨ Your wallet is ready. To import a private key or keystore file, or to
watch an address, use **Settings → Accounts** afterwards.

<Warning>
Your recovery phrase is your money. Anyone who has it can take your LAX,
and no one, including Parallax, can recover it for you. Read
[Keeping your wallet safe](../wallets/keep-safe).
</Warning>

## Around the app

- **The sidebar** — your current account at the top (click it to switch
  accounts), then **Overview**, **Send**, **Receive** and **History**;
  **Contacts**; and **Sign message** and **Verify message**. **Settings**
  sits at the bottom.
- **Overview** — your balance, what's waiting to be confirmed, and your
  recent activity.
- **The node bar** — the capsule at the bottom of the window shows
  whether your node is *Syncing*, *Synced* or *Offline*, its latest
  block, its peers and how long it has been up. Its button connects or
  disconnects the node. Click the bar to open the **Network** panel:
  the block clock, the latest blocks, your peers (where you can add,
  trust or block one), the mempool, and more details about your node.
- **The top bar** — notifications, **Lock wallet**, and an update button
  when a new version is ready.

## Accounts

Your accounts are grouped by kind, in the account switcher and in
**Settings → Accounts**:

| Kind | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Recovery phrases** | Accounts from a 12 or 24-word phrase. One phrase can hold many accounts: **New account from this phrase** adds the next one. |
| **Single keys** | One account from a private key or a keystore file. Its backup is the keystore file, which **Save backup** writes where you choose. |
| **Hardware wallets** | Accounts whose keys stay on a Ledger or Trezor. |
| **Watch-only** | An address you follow without its key, such as one kept on an [offline signer](#offline-signing). |

From **Settings → Accounts** you can create and import accounts, rename
them, show a recovery phrase (it asks for your password), save a backup,
and remove accounts. An account with no backup yet is marked **No
backup**.

For more about how accounts and addresses work, see
[Accounts & addresses](../wallets/accounts).

## Sending and receiving

- **Receive** shows your address, a **Copy** button and a QR code. Only
  send LAX on the Parallax network (chain ID 2110) to it.
- **Send** takes an address or a contact's name, and an amount (**Max**
  sends your whole balance less the fee). The network fee is
  **Suggested** by your node, or **Custom** if you'd rather set it. You
  review the fee and the total before anything is sent, and the app warns
  you when something looks off, such as sending to yourself or paying far
  more than needed.
- A send that's still waiting can be sped up, or cancelled, from its row
  in your activity: both replace it with the same transaction (or 0 LAX
  to yourself) at a higher fee.
- **History** lists everything you've sent, received and mined, with
  filters and search, and **Export** saves it as a CSV file.
- **Contacts** is your address book: names, notes and favourites, which
  you can import and export as JSON or CSV.

Sending needs your node running and in sync. More in
[Sending and receiving LAX](../wallets/send-and-reveive).

## Signing messages

**Sign message** proves you own an address by signing a short message
with it; **Verify message** checks someone else's signed message. The
app uses the standard personal-message signature (EIP-191), so other
wallets can verify it too.

## Offline signing

For the most careful setups, Parallax Wallet can keep your keys on a
computer that never goes online.

1. On the offline computer, install Parallax Wallet and choose **Offline
   signer** at first launch (or turn it on in **Settings → General**).
   It never starts a node or checks for updates.
2. Create or import your account there. Choose **Show for watch-only** to
   show its address as a QR code.
3. On your online computer, add that address as a **watch-only** account.
4. To send, use **Send** on the online computer: it makes a signing
   request, as a QR code or a file. Scan or open it on the offline
   signer, check it, and sign. Bring the signed transaction back and
   **Broadcast** it.

Only plain LAX transfers can be signed offline, and **Fee cap** in
**Settings → General** limits the fee the signer will accept.

## Keeping it locked

- The wallet unlocks with your password once per session, and **locks
  itself** after 15 minutes of inactivity. Change that in **Settings →
  Security → Lock automatically**, or lock it at any time from the top
  bar. On macOS and Windows it also locks when your computer sleeps or
  its screen locks.
- Showing a recovery phrase, saving a backup, removing an account and
  changing your password always ask for your password again.
- **Settings → Security → Change password** re-encrypts every key and
  phrase. Backups saved before the change still open only with the old
  password.
- Everything the wallet keeps beside your keys, such as account names,
  contacts and the app's logs, is encrypted too.

## Settings

| Section | What's there |
|---|---|
| **General** | Language, open at login, starting the node automatically, keeping the computer awake while syncing, notifications, automatic update checks, and offline signing |
| **Appearance** | Theme (System, Light or Dark), account avatars, and turning off animations |
| **Storage** | Where the wallet and the node's data live, moving them, and wiping the wallet |
| **Accounts** | Creating, importing, backing up and removing accounts |
| **Security** | Your wallet password and automatic locking |
| **Node** | Sync mode (*Snap*, recommended, or *Full*), caches, fee estimation and the transaction pool |
| **Network** | Inbound connections, peers, port mapping, and privacy options such as Tor |
| **RPC** | The node's HTTP-RPC (on by default, at `127.0.0.1:8545`), WebSocket and other interfaces |
| **AI assistants** | Letting AI assistants use your node and wallet, and what they may do; see [AI assistants in Parallax Wallet](../ai/parallax-wallet) |
| **Diagnostics** | Log level and the app's live logs |
| **About** | The app's version, checking for updates, and ways to report a problem or request a feature |

Changes take effect when you **Save**; the few that need the node or the
app to restart say so. Advanced options show the command-line flag they
match, if you also use the [CLI client](./setup).

<Note>
Your node's HTTP-RPC is what other wallets connect to. To use MetaMask
with your own node, point it at `http://127.0.0.1:8545`; see
[Setting up MetaMask](../wallets/metamask). It only listens on your own
computer unless you change its listen address.
</Note>

## Updates

Parallax Wallet checks for a new version every 30 minutes (turn this off
in **Settings → General**), or when you press **Check for updates** in
**Settings → About**. When one is out, an update button appears in the
top bar. The app downloads it and checks it against the release's
`SHA256SUMS.txt` before installing:

- **Windows and Linux** — it installs the update and restarts when you
  say so.
- **macOS** — it opens the new version's disk image; drag Parallax
  Wallet to *Applications* to replace the old one.

An offline signer never checks for updates: install new versions by
hand.

## Files on your computer

The node's data (the blockchain) goes in the same places as the CLI
client's, unless you chose another folder:

- **Windows**: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Parallax` (or `%APPDATA%\Parallax` if an
  older install already uses it)
- **macOS**: `~/Library/Parallax`
- **Linux**: `~/.parallax`

The app's settings (`gui.json`) and your wallet (its encrypted keys,
phrases, contacts and logs) live in a `Parallax` folder in your system's
app-config folder:

- **Windows**: `%APPDATA%\Parallax`
- **macOS**: `~/Library/Application Support/Parallax`
- **Linux**: `~/.config/Parallax`

**Settings → Storage** shows both, and can move them. Because the node
uses the same data directory as the CLI client, you can switch between
them without downloading the chain again.

<Warning>
These folders are not a backup. If this computer is lost or broken, what
brings your accounts back is your recovery phrases, written on paper, and
your single keys' backup files, saved with **Save backup**.
</Warning>

## Getting help

In **Settings → About**, **Report a problem** and **Request a feature**
open a short form on GitHub, with the app's version filled in. Never
share your recovery phrase, password or a private key there, or anywhere
else.
